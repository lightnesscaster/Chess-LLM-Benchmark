Methodology

Static-position benchmark used to seed ratings before a model plays its first game

Each model chooses a move in 50 roughly equal positions, scored by Stockfish at depth 30. Centipawn loss, best-move rate, and legal-move rate predict a game rating: 1298.57 − 200.43·ln(CPL+1) + 15.39·best% + 5.85·survival40. A game-like panel and continuation-stability probe can only lower that prediction.
